# Privacy-safe original homeowner quote research pilot

**Status: not collecting submissions. Public verified sample size = 0.**
Blank template: /templates/standby-quote-pilot-blank.csv

## Pilot design
- Obtain separate consent before research data collection and separately before
  publication of anonymized statistics.
- Distinguish quotes, completed invoices, allowances and revisions. Never count
  bids for the same household as independent observations.
- Require exact model/fuel/ATS and a scope-completeness checklist; no invented
  splits of bundled totals.
- Intake backend MUST be private with authentication, rate limiting, file-type
  and malware inspection, encryption, access logs, scheduled deletion and an
  identity-aware withdrawal/correction mechanism BEFORE uploads are opened.
- Never collect or publish full address, full ZIP, photos with people,
  electrical account numbers, signatures or banking/payment details.
- Private document proof and fingerprints never appear in public exports.
- Data protection: document retention, opt-in, data minimization, access limits,
  correction and deletion before enrollment; have legal/privacy review.
- Publish only with confirmed consent, verified redaction, review logs and
  a predeclared analysis plan. The editorial minimum of 20 independent verified
  observations is a suppression threshold, not statistical representativeness.
- Show n, periods, geography, configuration, missing scope and uncertainty.
  Never fabricate real homeowners or claim a national median from convenience data.

## Immediate deliverables available
Local three-quote planner, blank private-use CSV, explanation of exact scope and
limitations, public no-research-yet disclosure. There is NO live research form or
back end today. The user must approve and fund the compliant infrastructure
before genuine cohort collection can begin.
